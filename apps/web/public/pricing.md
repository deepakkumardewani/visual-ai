# Visual AI pricing

Credits only. No paid subscription tiers. Image studio only (no video product).

Interactive checkout: [https://visual-ai.app/pricing](https://visual-ai.app/pricing)

## Included credits

| Kind      | Amount         | Persistence                  |
| --------- | -------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Signup    | 50 credits     | Persistent — do not expire   |
| Daily     | 30 credits     | Reset each day (use-or-lose) |
| Purchased | Per pack below | Persistent — never expire    |

Daily credits do not roll over. Bought credits stack on top and stay until you spend them.

## What credits buy

Shown in the studio before you generate. Bands:

- Budget / standard image generation: **1 credit**
- Premium generation (FLUX Pro/Max and similar): **4–5 credits**
- Utility — upscale, colorize, restore, remove background: **2 credits**
- Premium upscalers (Clarity Pro, Topaz): **4 credits**

## Tools

Image studio only: generate, upscale, colorize, restore, remove background. FLUX family including FLUX Pro and FLUX Max, plus photo tools.

You own the images you make. Commercial use is allowed.
