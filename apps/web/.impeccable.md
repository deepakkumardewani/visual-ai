## Design Context

### Users

Everyday consumers restoring personal photographs — faded family portraits, damaged memories, black-and-white snapshots they want to colorize. They arrive with an emotional motive, not a technical one. Context: late-night browsing on phone or laptop, often nostalgic and cautious about trusting an AI tool with precious images.

### Brand Personality

Warm, tactile, reverent. The product should feel like a careful restorer's studio — not a flashy AI demo. Emotional goals: trust, tenderness, quiet delight when they see their photo come back to life.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark editorial gallery. Warm near-black canvas lets restored photographs carry the color. Single copper accent used sparingly for CTAs. Typography: Young Serif (display) + Source Sans 3 (body) — heritage warmth without template serif clichés. Anti-references: purple→pink gradients, generic AI startup hero, Material purple chips, gradient text, centered card grids.

### Design Principles

1. Photos are the color — UI recedes into warm neutrals.
2. One accent, used like a wax seal — rare and intentional.
3. Editorial rhythm — asymmetric layouts, varied spacing, left-aligned copy where possible.
4. Motion reveals meaning — entrances and scroll reveals, never decorative bounce.
5. Honest copy and trust signals — no fabricated metrics or testimonials.
